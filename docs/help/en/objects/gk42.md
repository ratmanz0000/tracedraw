# GK-42 calibration

[[docker.gk42]] ties the page millimetres to true GK-42 metres (the
Gauss-Kruger grid of 1942) with three reference markers on the page. The
three markers give the turn of the map, a scale along each axis and the
offset, so a map that a scan or a redraw turned or stretched a little is
still usable. A calibration saved with two markers by an earlier version
keeps working with the axis-only model (no turn).

The canvas keeps only the markers and the marks of the picked points;
everything else lives in the [[docker.gk42]], and the [[toolbar.gk42]]
button on the standard bar turns the mode on and off.

## Placing the markers {#placing}

1. Press [[gk42.place_points]] in the docker (or the [[toolbar.gk42]]
   button).
2. Click on the page: the first click sets marker 1 (red), the second
   marker 2 (blue), the third marker 3 (amber). The three markers must not
   lie on one straight line, and their order matters: marker 1 and marker 2
   are the ends of the map's diagonal, marker 3 is the corner that completes
   the triangle.
3. Type the true GK-42 coordinates of the three markers in the table, in
   metres, and press [[gk42.apply]].

The markers are small circles on the [[gk42.layer]] layer of the page.
Move them with any tool and the calibration follows at once. The
coordinates accept thousand groups (6 519 000), a comma or a dot as the
decimal mark.

While the mode is on every further click on the page adds a picked
point: a row in the table with the page millimetres, the GK-42 metres
and the distance to the previous point in map metres (the first row
measures from marker 1). [[gk42.clear]] removes the picked rows,
[[gk42.copy]] puts the whole table on the clipboard, [[gk42.csv]] as
semicolon separated values.

## The scales {#scales}

The [[gk42.scales]] block shows the two scales as 1:N, the zone number
taken from the leading digits of the eastings, and the deformation: how
much the Y scale differs from the X one, in percent. The distance column
measures along both scales and the turn. A calibration whose markers are
gone or stand on one line is reported in red; apply the coordinates again
or [[gk42.reset]].

The calibration is saved in the drawing and comes back with it;
[[gk42.reset]] takes it and the markers away in one undo step.

## Export a layer to Excel

Right-click a layer in the Objects docker and choose [[gk42.export_layer]].
The workbook has one row per object directly on the layer. The first cell
contains the object name, the text itself, or the text from inside a group.
The other two cells hold Y (easting) and X (northing) in whole metres.

The coordinates mark the centre of the object's bounds. For a group, they
use the first symbol instance found in drawing order, or the first immediate
subgroup without a text object in its subtree if there is no symbol.
A group without either leaves its coordinate cells empty.
